Frequently asked questions

What is a NOM, and when does it apply?

Normas Oficiales Mexicanas are mandatory safety, labelling or performance requirements certain products must meet before being sold in Mexico. We check whether yours is subject to one.

Do I need a Mexican company to sell here?

Not always. You can operate through an importer or a local representative; we can also invoice and represent your product commercially in Mexico.

Why does tariff classification matter?

The tariff code determines the duties, permits and requirements that apply to your product. Getting it wrong costs money and time at the border.

What kind of products do you work with?

Consumer and industrial goods from European manufacturers. Our own brands work in window coverings and in food.
