How we work

First we get to know your product and your plans

We don't sell ready-made packages. The work depends on the product, the target market and what the company already has in place. That's why every project starts with a conversation and an analysis, and only then do we present a concrete solution, scope and quote.

  1. Free consultation

    We get to know your company, products and plans for Mexico. We talk through the options and tell you openly where we can help. The consultation is free and without obligation.

  2. Product analysis

    We ask for technical data sheets, composition, packaging photos and any certificates you have. On that basis we analyse the product requirements, tariff classification, NOM standards and labelling rules.

  3. Proposal and quote

    We set out the next steps, the scope of our work, who is responsible for what and an expected timeline. Our fee and third-party costs, such as testing, certification or inspection fees, are shown separately in the quote.

  4. Product preparation

    We manage the preparation of your product for Mexico: we coordinate tariff classification, certification, labelling and the work with the customs broker, laboratories and certification bodies. We keep you updated throughout.

  5. First shipment

    We help you choose the import model and the right partners, prepare the paperwork and organise transport. We coordinate the first clearance and delivery to the location you specify in Mexico.

  6. Sales and growth

    Once the product is ready, we can help you find distributors and customers, prepare for trade shows and support further sales growth. If needed, we can also act as your company's local partner in Mexico.

Who we work with

Some steps can only be carried out by authorised parties. Customs clearance is handled by licensed customs brokers, while certification and testing are done by accredited bodies and laboratories in Mexico. We work with trusted partners and choose them according to the product and the project.

For you, that means a single point of contact. We coordinate the work, gather the information, follow each stage and keep you up to date on how the project is going.

Let's start with a conversation about your product

The first consultation is free. Tell us what you make and how you picture selling in Mexico, and we'll tell you what needs to be prepared and where we can help. We only put a proposal together once we understand your product.
