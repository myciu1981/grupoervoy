Our brands

We build our own brands in Mexico

Alongside our work with European manufacturers, we develop our own brands in Mexico. We go through the same stages we handle for clients: preparing the product and meeting local requirements, import and logistics, and building sales. Our experience comes not only from consulting but above all from practice.

Simple Made Blinds Mexico

Made-to-measure shading systems and blinds for homes, offices and property developments in Mexico. The brand combines European solutions with local manufacturing and installation.

Polaca Foods

Central European food products for Mexican shops, wholesalers and the food service sector. Polaca Foods is building an import channel that brings products from different European manufacturers to Mexico.
