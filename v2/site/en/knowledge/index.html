Frequently asked questions

What is a NOM and when does it apply?
Normas Oficiales Mexicanas are mandatory Mexican technical regulations covering, among other things, product safety, technical specifications and labelling. Not every product is subject to the same requirements: which standards apply depends, among other things, on the type of product and its classification. We check this at the analysis stage.
Do I need a company in Mexico to sell my products there?
Not always. Depending on the product and the sales model, you can start by working with an importer, a distributor or another local partner. In some cases we can also provide local support and represent your company under a contract. Setting up your own company makes sense when the scale of the business justifies a structure in Mexico.
Why does the tariff code matter so much?
Tariff classification affects, among other things, the duty rate, regulatory requirements and the documents needed for import. That's why it's worth settling and verifying it before the first shipment.
What kinds of products do you work with?
We work with consumer and industrial products made in Europe. We don't limit ourselves to one industry. We look at every project individually, and in the first conversation we tell you honestly whether we can really help.
How much does it cost to start selling in Mexico?
It depends mainly on the type of product and the requirements it has to meet before going on sale. Some products mainly need correct labelling; others also need certification, testing or permits. That's why we prepare a proposal and quote only after analysing the product. The first consultation is free.
How long does it take to prepare a product for sale in Mexico?
It depends on the product, the documents required, certification and the external bodies involved. After the analysis we give you an expected timeline and point out what could affect the date sales can start.
Can you act as the importer of my goods?
We don't act as importer. What we do is help you define the right import model, choose a partner who can take on that role and coordinate the first shipment with them.
Which languages can we speak?
English, Spanish and Polish. With a team in Mexico and Poland, we can work easily with the manufacturer in Europe and with local partners in Mexico.
How do we get started?
Book a free consultation and write a few lines about your product and your plans for Mexico. In the conversation we'll agree what you need, then ask for the documents required for the analysis and prepare a proposal for the next steps.
