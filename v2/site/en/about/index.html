About

A Mexican company connecting Europe with the local market

GRUPO ERVOY is a Mexican company based in Monterrey, Nuevo León, with a team working in Mexico and Poland. That's why we understand both sides: the European manufacturer, with its way of working and its expectations, and the realities of the Mexican market.

MonterreyHead office in Nuevo León

3 languagesSpanish, English and Polish
2 own brandsSimple Made Blinds Mexico and Polaca Foods

Our experience started with our own brands. With Simple Made Blinds Mexico and Polaca Foods we went through product preparation, regulatory requirements, import, logistics and building sales ourselves. Today we use that experience to help other European manufacturers grow in Mexico.

Why Monterrey

Monterrey is one of Mexico's main industrial and business centres. Being in the north of the country gives us good access to the northern market, the United States, the main business hubs and transport infrastructure. It's a natural base for growing in the Mexican market.

Our approach

Before proposing anything specific, we want to understand the product, the business model and the company's plans. Only then can we judge what you really need and where we can help. If we think Mexico isn't the right market for your product yet, we'll tell you straight.

We don't believe in one-size-fits-all packages. That's why we start with a free conversation and prepare a specific proposal only after getting to know the product and the support it needs.
