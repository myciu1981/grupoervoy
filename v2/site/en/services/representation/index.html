Your local partner in Mexico

Not every company needs its own entity in Mexico from day one. In many cases it's easier to start with a local partner who can act on the ground and support the company's growth.

How it works

Under a contract, we can represent your company within an agreed scope: with trading partners, certain institutions and the parties involved in preparing and selling the product. The scope of representation is always defined case by case.

This lets you start operating in Mexico without building your own structure straight away, and decide on the next step once the business starts to grow.

Local presence

Within the scope set out in the contract, we can support your company with local formal and organisational matters and coordinate work with the relevant parties in Mexico.

Sales support

We can also represent your company with customers, distributors and potential partners: presenting your offer, answering questions, attending meetings and supporting negotiations.

If you later want your own company

When the scale of the business justifies your own structure in Mexico, we can help prepare that step and hand over selected tasks to your company smoothly.

Let's start with a conversation about your product

The first consultation is free. Tell us what you make and how you see your sales in Mexico, and we'll tell you what needs to be prepared and where we can help. We only put a proposal together once we understand your product.
