Services

Getting your product ready for sale in Mexico

Before the product goes on sale, you need to check which requirements apply in Mexico and prepare everything needed to place it on the market legally.

Tariff classification

The tariff code affects, among other things, the duty rate, regulatory requirements and any permits. We prepare a preliminary classification and then verify it with a customs broker. For more complex products we also help prepare the documentation needed to obtain an official ruling.

Mexican NOM standards and certification

We establish which requirements and standards apply to the product and check whether it can be placed on the market as it is. If certification is needed, we help choose the right body, prepare the documentation, arrange testing and coordinate the process through to the end.

Depending on the product and the business model, certification may require a party registered in Mexico. We go through the options at the start, because they shape how the product will be imported and sold.

Spanish labelling

We check what information must appear on the packaging or label and prepare the Spanish version to meet Mexican requirements. Where needed, we also coordinate confirmation of compliance with the relevant inspection unit.

Who does what

Part of the work we do ourselves. Where accreditation or a licence is required, we work with customs brokers, laboratories and accredited certification and inspection bodies. We coordinate the whole process, so you don't have to manage several suppliers yourself.

What you get

At the end you receive an organised set of documents and information needed to import and sell the product in Mexico.

Let's start with a conversation about your product

The first consultation is free. Tell us what you make and how you see your sales in Mexico, and we'll tell you what needs to be prepared and where we can help. We only put a proposal together once we understand your product.
